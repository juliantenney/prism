## Discipline-sensitive review note

This validation demonstrates that **evidence-richness criteria must be interpreted according to the discipline**.

For **introductory engineering mathematics**, appropriate evidence criteria include:

- circuit schematics
- measurement records
- component specifications
- operational constraints
- calculated and observed values
- alternative circuit configurations
- model assumptions and limitations

The following should **not** automatically be treated as required for this resource type:

- research-source diversity
- competing scholarly interpretations
- publication evaluation
- disciplinary controversy

Generic humanities-oriented evidence criteria were **rejected** where they did not align with the engineering outcomes (see Rejected F_R02–F_R05).
