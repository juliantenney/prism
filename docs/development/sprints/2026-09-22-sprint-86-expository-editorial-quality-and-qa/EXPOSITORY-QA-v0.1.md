# Expository QA v0.1

**Status:** **PRE-VALIDATION instrument** (S86-T-004)  
**Not accepted as final framework** — criteria must remain stable while evaluating cases 02+  
**Companion:** [WP1-FINDINGS.md](WP1-FINDINGS.md) · [VALIDATION-SET.md](VALIDATION-SET.md) · [DRAFT-EDITORIAL-PRINCIPLES.md](DRAFT-EDITORIAL-PRINCIPLES.md)

This process is a **diagnostic instrument**, not an optimisation target. Do **not** use numerical scores. Human editorial judgement is first-class evidence.

---

## A. Evaluation inputs

**Prefer (primary):**

1. Learner-facing rendered/exported resource (HTML package or equivalent standalone export).  
2. Creation context when needed for fairness (topic, audience, intended extent, product type).  
3. Source/grounding material when the resource claims fidelity to sources.

**Use sparingly (diagnostic only):**

4. Internal workflow artefacts (EJP/XD/XM/DP captures) — only when diagnosing *where* a learner-facing problem likely arises; **not** required to record that a learner-facing problem exists.

**Do not** judge editorial quality primarily from plans or prompts.

---

## B. Evaluation procedure (repeatable)

Practical sequence — omit a pass only when clearly N/A:

1. **Context note** — record product, topic, audience, intended extent (from creation context).  
2. **Whole-resource first read** — read as a learner; note global impressions before analytic dissection.  
3. **Intellectual journey pass** — instructional architecture: order, sufficiency, missing/confused moves.  
4. **Section explanatory pass** — sample or review sections for clarity, density, forward momentum.  
5. **Representation pass** — purpose and prose/representation integration.  
6. **Editorial / rhetorical pass** — entry, pacing, emphasis, recurrence, metacommentary, closure.  
7. **Learner-facing information architecture pass** — which labelled regions exist; whether they earn their place.  
8. **Typography / readability pass** — hierarchy, measure/rhythm, maths/figure relationships (requirements-level; not font fashion).  
9. **Grounding / formal correctness pass** — where applicable (claims, maths, sources).  
10. **Defect triage** — separate **functional/rendering defects** from editorial findings.  
11. **Synthesis** — answer the synthesis questions in §F without a numeric total.

Keep notes short. Prefer evidence quotes / structural observations over vague taste language.

## C. QA dimensions (v0.1)

Refined from the charter provisional list. Synonyms collapsed. Each dimension is judged independently where applicable.

### D1 — Intellectual journey
- **Judges:** Whether the resource constructs understanding in a coherent progressive order for the audience.  
- **Strong:** Necessary distinctions appear when needed; later sections depend productively on earlier ones.  
- **Failure modes:** Missing threshold moves; premature formalism; arbitrary section order; journey only announced not enacted.  
- **Evidence:** Section sequence; dependency of later explanations on earlier ones.  
- **Layer:** Primarily **instructional architecture**.

### D2 — Explanatory sufficiency
- **Judges:** Whether difficult ideas receive enough explanation for the stated audience/extent.  
- **Strong:** Hard distinctions are worked; easy material is not over-explained relative to extent.  
- **Failure modes:** Handwaving at thresholds; padding on routine points; audience mismatch.  
- **Evidence:** Treatment of known hard concepts vs routine ones.  
- **Layer:** Instructional (with editorial density implications).

### D3 — Editorial coherence & reading experience
- **Judges:** Whether the resource feels authored as one intellectual experience rather than assembled components.  
- **Strong:** Entry, body, and close belong to one voice and arc; joints create momentum.  
- **Failure modes:** Prospectus tone; disjointed modules; repeated “restarting.”  
- **Evidence:** Whole-resource first read; transition quality.  
- **Layer:** **Editorial architecture**.

### D4 — Rhetorical quality & selective emphasis
- **Judges:** Variation, emphasis, and restraint in prose.  
- **Strong:** Threshold moments carry more weight; routine moves are quieter; limited metacommentary.  
- **Failure modes:** Even teacherly cadence; claim→explain→restate loops; explaining the explanation.  
- **Evidence:** Marked sentences; paragraph rhythm; restatement counts.  
- **Layer:** Editorial.

### D5 — Representation purpose
- **Judges:** Whether artefacts exist because they explain something particularly well.  
- **Strong:** Removing the artefact would damage understanding.  
- **Failure modes:** Decorative or checklist visuals; unused commissions.  
- **Evidence:** Artefact role relative to the argument.  
- **Layer:** Both (commission instructional; placement editorial).  
- **N/A:** When the resource legitimately needs few/no graphics.

### D6 — Prose–representation integration
- **Judges:** Whether prose and artefacts compose one explanation.  
- **Strong:** Exposition moves into and out of the artefact; artefact is used, not merely adjacent.  
- **Failure modes:** Figure dumped after a self-contained prose block; caption restates prose without advancing.  
- **Evidence:** Cross-references, sequencing, mid-argument use.  
- **Layer:** Editorial (+ pipeline seam).  
- **N/A:** When no representations are present.

### D7 — Recurrence vs redundancy
- **Judges:** Whether repeated ideas develop or merely repeat.  
- **Strong:** Recurrence adds qualification, formalisation, comparison, application, or integration.  
- **Failure modes:** Front matter / body / closing saying the same thing; synonym restatement.  
- **Evidence:** Parallel passages across regions.  
- **Layer:** Editorial.

### D8 — Learner-facing information architecture
- **Judges:** Which internal artefacts appear as labelled learner furniture and whether they earn that visibility.  
- **Strong:** Visible structure serves reading; internal fields are not auto-projected.  
- **Failure modes:** Stacked Overview / Purpose / Summary / Closing prospectus; machinery labels.  
- **Evidence:** Orientation/closing regions; labelled headings.  
- **Layer:** Editorial (Design Page / renderer surface).

### D9 — Opening / intellectual entry
- **Judges:** How the resource creates reason to engage.  
- **Strong:** Subject-appropriate entry (problem, tension, case, contrast, phenomenon, etc.).  
- **Failure modes:** Administrative “this resource will cover…” as the primary entry.  
- **Evidence:** First screenful / first section.  
- **Layer:** Editorial (+ EJP hypothesis later).  
- **Note:** Does **not** require a provocative question.

### D10 — Closure / synthesis
- **Judges:** How the resource ends the intellectual experience.  
- **Strong:** Shows what can now be understood that could not at the start; preferably one consolidating close.  
- **Failure modes:** Multiple competing conclusions; mechanical section checklist; restated prospectus.  
- **Evidence:** Final section(s) + page closing.  
- **Layer:** Editorial.

### D11 — Audience appropriateness
- **Judges:** Register, prerequisites, and example world fit the stated audience.  
- **Strong:** Accessible without condescension; formalism introduced when prepared.  
- **Failure modes:** Jargon dump; fake simplicity that hides the hard move.  
- **Evidence:** Examples, definitions, pacing of formalism.  
- **Layer:** Both.

### D12 — Grounding / fidelity
- **Judges:** Faithfulness to authorised claims/sources where relevant.  
- **Strong:** Qualifications preserved; no invented authority.  
- **Failure modes:** Overclaiming; silent invention; dropped hedges.  
- **Evidence:** Claims vs brief/sources.  
- **N/A:** Pure conceptual resources without external source claims.  
- **Layer:** Instructional / fidelity.

### D13 — Formal / mathematical correctness
- **Judges:** Notation and formal relations where present.  
- **Strong:** Correct, consistent, audience-appropriate formalism.  
- **Failure modes:** Corrupted notation; false precision; inconsistent symbols.  
- **Evidence:** Equations, worked quantities.  
- **N/A:** Non-formal resources.  
- **Layer:** Instructional / correctness.

### D14 — Typography / readability / hierarchy
- **Judges:** Whether presentation reveals intellectual structure.  
- **Strong:** Clear hierarchy; readable measure/rhythm; maths/figures related to prose; restrained chrome.  
- **Failure modes:** Flat heading levels; UI-widget feel; decorative panels competing with argument.  
- **Evidence:** Heading tree; layout observations.  
- **Layer:** Editorial (renderer).  
- **Note:** Not “make it pretty.”

### D15 — Accessibility baseline
- **Judges:** Ordinary semantic structure and accessible alternatives remain intact.  
- **Strong:** Sensible headings, alt text, reading order.  
- **Failure modes:** Broken semantics; missing alternatives for essential figures.  
- **Evidence:** Structure / alt / order.  
- **Layer:** Cross-cutting (not editorial flourish).

### Defect channel (not a quality dimension)
**Functional / rendering defects** (e.g. unsupported structured-material fallback) are recorded separately from D1–D15 judgements so they do not masquerade as editorial mediocrity.

## D. Judgement convention

### Quality rating (per applicable dimension)

| Rating | Meaning |
| ------ | ------- |
| **STRONG** | Clearly supports a high-quality authored-chapter experience on this dimension |
| **SATISFACTORY** | Acceptable; not a limiting factor |
| **CONCERN** | Materially limits editorial/instructional quality; evidence cited |
| **N/A** | Dimension does not apply to this resource |

### Severity (for CONCERN findings only)

| Severity | Meaning |
| -------- | ------- |
| **High** | Dominates the reading experience or blocks understanding |
| **Medium** | Repeated or structurally important drag |
| **Low** | Local / occasional |

Applicability is expressed via **N/A**, not by forcing a weak score.

No numeric totals. No averaging.

## E. Finding format

Record each material finding as:

```text
FINDING-ID: S86-Cxx-Fnn
DIMENSION: D#
RATING: STRONG | SATISFACTORY | CONCERN | N/A
SEVERITY: High | Medium | Low | — (if not CONCERN)
OBSERVATION: …
EVIDENCE: … (quote, structural location, figure id, etc.)
LEARNER/EDITORIAL CONSEQUENCE: …
OWNERSHIP HINT: … (optional; only if reasonably inferable — EJP/XD/XM/DP/assembly/renderer/functional)
```

Ownership hints are **optional diagnostics**, never required to legitimise a learner-facing finding.

## F. Overall synthesis (no numeric total)

After dimensional notes, answer:

1. **What is working?** (especially instructional strengths)  
2. **What most limits this resource from feeling like a high-quality authored chapter?**  
3. **Which findings appear systemic vs case-specific?**  
4. **What requires further evidence?** (including principles/dimensions to challenge next)

Optional one-line overall stance (not a score):

> Instructional architecture: STRONG / SATISFACTORY / CONCERN  
> Editorial architecture: STRONG / SATISFACTORY / CONCERN
