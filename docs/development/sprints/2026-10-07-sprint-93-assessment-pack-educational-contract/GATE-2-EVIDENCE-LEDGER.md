## Cross-case notes (EVIDENCE — not final product architecture)

Recorded explicitly as Gate 2 evidence (authorised findings 1–10):

1. Across all three journeys, Assessment Pack is commissioned where the principal educational job becomes **deliberate elicitation of interpretable evidence of an integrated learner capability**.
2. Elicitation, evidence capture, feedback, judgement, reflection, revision, evaluation, independence, or authentic performance do **not** individually imply Assessment Pack.
3. Interactive remains appropriate when consequential reasoning/action/revision is principally the mechanism by which capability is being **developed**.
4. Situated Task remains appropriate when carrying out purposeful activity in authentic context is the principal learning vehicle, even when that activity generates rich evidence used later.
5. Assessment Pack demand includes **sustained constructed performance** and cannot be reduced to conventional item/question forms.
6. A **determinate correct answer is not required**. High-quality performance may involve competing defensible conclusions, calibrated uncertainty, or a justified unresolved position.
7. Assessment Pack may **consume/reconnect** with learner work produced earlier without owning or recreating the activity that produced it (esp. Case B).
8. **Integrated performance can itself be educationally important**; atomising it into separately scaffolded questions may destroy the capability being elicited.
9. Feedback remains **formative**. Assessment Pack is nowhere near summative: no grades, certification, high-stakes decisions, formal marking/moderation, gradebooks, academic-integrity systems, or institutional assessment management.
10. Preserve the discrepancy: live LJ treats Assessment Pack as capable of sustained constructed performance; Gate 1 shows CURRENT implementation is closed-form, deterministic, auto-checkable — an **implementation / educational-contract gap**, not proof the capability already exists.
