## 5. Strongest features

**1. Instructional sequencing.** Learners first distinguish constrained from unconstrained optimisation, then construct a Lagrangian, solve a different system, and finally interpret multipliers economically. This separates conceptual, representational, procedural and interpretive demands while keeping their relationships visible.

**2. Diagnostic self-review.** Every activity contains four substantive review criteria rather than simple completion checking. For example, the solution activity asks learners to inspect derivative coverage, algebraic consistency, reported values and constraint verification, with specific corrective action for omissions. This strongly supports self-regulated revision.

**3. Model–practice separation.** Construction and solution activities model one optimisation problem and deliberately give learners a different problem for independent work. This reduces answer copying while retaining sufficient scaffolding to support transfer of the demonstrated process.

## 10. Preserve

1. **Preserve the Learn → Do → Check rhythm.** Its repeated structure reduces navigational overhead while progressively changing the disciplinary thinking required.

2. **Preserve criterion-based guided review rather than replacing it with automated free-text judgement.** The current approach gives learners observable diagnostic criteria and concrete revision actions, matching Prism's intended self-regulation model. 

3. **Preserve the restrained, text-first interaction design.** Editable workspaces, accessible navigation, explanatory visuals and progressive disclosure support the learning without unnecessary interface complexity, consistent with Prism's design principles. 

## 11. Final judgement

**Release ready with minor revisions.**

This is a coherent and technically sound introductory resource with strong constructive alignment and an especially effective diagnostic-review architecture. Its most valuable revisions concern disciplinary precision and transfer rather than redesign or additional interaction.

**Strongest feature:** Criterion-based self-review integrated consistently across all four activities.

**Most important improvement:** Tighten the distinction between satisfying first-order conditions, obtaining candidate solutions and establishing an optimal solution.

**Greatest educational risk:** Learners may carry simplified introductory representations of optimality or shadow prices into contexts where additional conditions matter.
