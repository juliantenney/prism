## S105-D02 — Stage Authority, Progressive Capture, and Deterministic Assembly Contract

**Date:** 2026-11-25  
**Status:** **Accepted** (Phase 105A approved with refinements 1–9 below; Milestone 1 implemented and stopped for review)

### 1. Binding architectural refinements

1. **Immutable final Design Page contract:** The existing Learning Journey Design Page schema (`lib/learning-journey-design-page.js`) is immutable for Sprint 105. Intermediate capture schemas derive from it, not the other way round. **Zero intermediate-only fields** may leak into the assembled final Design Page. Downstream consumers (`lib/first-class-commission-intake.js`, `lib/learning-journey-unified-shell.js`, `lib/learning-journey-learner-package.js`, `lib/learning-journey-shared-source.js`, `lib/learning-journey-workload-feasibility-adapter.js`) remain unchanged.
2. **Minimum sufficient structured information:** Each stage outputs all and only the information required by its educational purpose, subsequent stages, and deterministic final assembly (no mechanical prose-heading-to-JSON translation). Every intermediate field has an explicit purpose and consumer (table below).
3. **Distinguish reasoning from capture:** Models undertake full educational reasoning while capturing only consequential decisions, constraints, and rationale needed subsequently. Stage ownership is enforced structurally in M1 and semantic economy via prompt guidance in M2, without arbitrary word limits.
4. **Single-stage authority & deterministic transport:** Each stage owns its authoritative information. Later stages must never regenerate earlier authoritative fields or anticipate later decisions. Established authoritative data is transported deterministically by the assembler wherever possible — including **Stage 1 `learning_outcomes`** (deterministically injected into `commissions[].journey_context_text`) and **brief-provided authoritative source material** (Stage 4 declares `{ status: "provided" }` without copying the corpus; assembler transports `briefProvidedSourceContext` verbatim into `shared_authoritative_material`).
5. **Complete progressive assembly:** **All five** accepted, valid, current stage captures (`learning_requirements`, `learning_progression`, `learning_elements`, `learning_commissions`, `design_page`) are **mandatory**. Stages 2 and 3 are authoritative educational design stages, not optional diagnostic inputs.
6. **Strict legacy separation:** Legacy monolithic workflows remain supported, **but monolithic Step 5 capture must never provide a validation bypass for explicitly progressive workflows** (`progressive_capture_version: "s105_v1"` / `outputSpec.partialPageOutputs: true`).
7. **Stored captures are authoritative (binding for M2/M3):** Accepted structured captures take precedence over conversational context, particularly following author edits. M2/M3 prompt handoff and invalidation must honour stored captures without unnecessary input duplication or reliance on superseded conversational state.
8. **Preserve existing educational & operational contracts:** Retain all Sprint 104 safeguards, authoritative durations (`duration_contract_version: "s104_m1"`), outcomes, commission specifications, continuity, shared source fidelity, and Journal semantics.
9. **Preserve Economics evidence:** Original baseline and incomplete regeneration remain untouched; no recovery or migration mechanism is built; live validation runs on a fresh workflow.
