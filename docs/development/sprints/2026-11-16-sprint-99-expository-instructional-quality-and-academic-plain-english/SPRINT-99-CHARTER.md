## Mission

Investigate and improve the quality of standalone **Expository** resources.

**Central question:** Does the resource help a learner develop accurate, connected and usable understanding through clear explanation, well-chosen examples and appropriately demanding reasoning?

**North star (from Sprint 98 / Sprint 97):** All learning works towards **better judgement, better capability, or both**.

---

## Programme context

Sprint 98 improved Interactive instructional coherence, Academic Plain English (Interactive GAM path), M5 attempt-before-scaffold presentation, and a **global** typography standard (S98-D08). Expository is a pedagogically independent product family with its own journey/development/materials pipeline (EJP → XD → XM), not Interactive EP/DLA/GAM.

This sprint evaluates Expository on its own terms. It does **not** transplant Interactive attempt-before-scaffold patterns.

## Guardrails

- Do **not** impose Interactive attempt-before-scaffold on Expository.  
- Do **not** treat explanation-before-activity as a defect.  
- Do **not** introduce arbitrary word limits or prioritise brevity over conceptual completeness.  
- Do **not** move Knowledge Summary after exposition.  
- Do **not** introduce unnecessary schemas, renderer mechanisms or definitions areas.  
- Preserve legitimate structural formatting; enforce global discretionary-emphasis ban (S98-D08).  
- Do **not** modify Assessment Pack or Situated Task in Sprint 99.  
- Regeneration produces variable resources — not a strict controlled A/B.  
- Make code changes only where evidence identifies a sufficiently clear problem.
