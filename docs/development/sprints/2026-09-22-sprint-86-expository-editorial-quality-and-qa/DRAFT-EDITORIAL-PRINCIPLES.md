---

## Overarching definition

> A high-quality Expository Resource constructs the **commissioned understanding** in its **appropriate epistemic form**, and presents that understanding as **one authored intellectual experience** — progressive, selectively emphasised, representation-aware, and closed once — rather than as an assembly of individually competent instructional components and internal page furniture.

This revises the provisional Sprint 86 definition by adding **purpose / epistemic integrity** alongside authored reading experience.

---

## EQ1–EQ8 (successor model)

### EQ1 — Preserve purpose and epistemic form

**Normative statement:** Construct the understanding the resource was asked to construct, in the epistemic form that understanding requires (mechanism, causal explanation, conceptual distinction, interpretation, competing weighting, uncertainty, qualification, comparison, formal relation, or other subject-appropriate form). Topical coverage of a neighbouring lesson is not success.

**Protects against:** Neighbouring-brief drift; coherent chapters that answer a different intellectual question; collapsing disagreement into a single neat model; treating one schematic spine as universal.

**Evidence:** **C04** hard negative (historiographical disagreement → multi-level outbreak causation). **C05** positive (validity / truth / premise acceptability / non-deductive appraisal kept distinct). **C03** preserves mechanism-contrast form.

---

### EQ2 — Open into the commissioned intellectual need

**Normative statement:** Begin by giving the learner a subject-appropriate reason to engage with the *commissioned* problem — a precise conceptual question, tension, contrast, case, phenomenon, or other fitting device — rather than leading with administrative prospectus. Intellectual need is not drama, journalistic hooks, surprise anecdotes, or obligatory rhetorical questions.

**Protects against:** Administrative openings; prescribed “hook” devices; strong entry into the wrong problem.

**Evidence:** Body entries often strong (**C05** conceptual problem; **C03** explanatory gap; **C02** craft example) while all five still front-load Overview / Learning purpose / Knowledge summary. **C04** shows wrong-problem entry remains purpose-fit failure (**EQ1**).

---

### EQ3 — Progress with attention proportional to difficulty

**Normative statement:** Construct understanding in a coherent progressive order the learner can experience through the explanation. Allocate explanatory attention to difficult distinctions, threshold concepts, qualifications and other high-value moves rather than padding routine points or merely announcing the journey.

**Protects against:** Arbitrary section order; missing threshold moves; even topical coverage that under-explains the hard object; excessive journey metacommentary.

**Evidence:** Instructional journeys strong in **C01–C03, C05**. **C04** fails because the hard object (weighting disagreement) was not the journey’s object. Orientation metacommentary recurs in all five.

---

### EQ4 — Prefer development; allow productive anchor recurrence

**Normative statement:** Important ideas may recur, but recurrence should develop understanding. Distinguish: (1) conceptual-anchor recurrence that consolidates a central test or formulation; (2) development, qualification, comparison or application; (3) repetition of explanatory work already completed. Prefer (1)–(2); avoid (3). Accessible prose that restates meaning also shown in a figure is not automatically (3).

**Protects against:** Orientation / body / Closing synonym loops; reconsolidating figures that add no perceptibility; wrongly penalising useful conceptual anchors.

**Evidence:** Completed-work repetition systematic in orientation/Closing (**all five**). **C05** validity-test recurrence is productive. **C03** a11y clarification on prose–figure overlap.

---

### EQ5 — Make representations earn their place and participate

**Normative statement:** Diagrams, tables, equations, structured examples and other artefacts should be warranted because their form makes relationships, patterns, sequence, contrast, causal order or structure more readily perceptible. Compose prose and representations as one explanation where artefacts are used. Essential instructional meaning must remain available without exclusive dependence on seeing the artefact. Do not generate representations merely to fill a visual quota.

**Protects against:** Decorative or under-warranted graphics; low-visual over-generation; artefacts dumped after self-contained prose; trapping essential meaning only in images.

**Evidence:** **C02** over-generation / weak warrant; **C03** high-warrant mechanism figures; **C05** mild mixed warrant; integration softness recurring; a11y equivalence ≠ redundancy (**C03**).

**Judgement sequence:** warrant → explanatory value of form → integration.  
**Rejected heuristic:** justified only if removing the image removes essential understanding.

---

### EQ6 — Emphasise selectively and trust the reader

**Normative statement:** Give threshold contrasts, surprises, qualifications and resolutions more rhetorical weight than routine connective moves. Where understanding is established, do not restate it or explain the explanation. Additional words should buy additional understanding.

**Protects against:** Even teacherly cadence; claim→explain→restate loops; condescending metacommentary.

**Evidence:** Stronger in **C01/C02**; milder residual in **C03–C05**. Remains a quality target even when not escalated to CONCERN.

---

### EQ7 — Show learner value and intellectual structure — not internal machinery

**Normative statement:** Visible page regions and typographic hierarchy should help the learner see intellectual structure and value. Internal planning fields (outcomes, knowledge summaries, synthesis slots, etc.) must not automatically appear as labelled learner-facing furniture. Hierarchy below the section title should reveal groupings and threshold structure where the content warrants it.

**Protects against:** Stacked Overview / Learning purpose / Knowledge summary / Closing prospectus; flat H2-only bodies; UI-like presentation of educational publishing content.

**Evidence:** Labelled orientation + Closing furniture in **all five**; **0×H3** in all five; functional typography throughout.

---

### EQ8 — Close once, consolidating the developed understanding

**Normative statement:** End with a single authoritative consolidation of what the learner can now understand. Consolidation must fit the epistemic form: it may integrate a model, restate a conceptual test, or clarify why legitimate disagreement or uncertainty persists. Do not add a second page-level Closing that merely repeats completed work, and do not force false resolution for neatness.

**Protects against:** Dual closure; mechanical section recap; interpretive false resolution into one integrated answer; mistaking repetition for consolidation.

**Evidence:** Dual close in **all five**. **C05**: S5 consolidates; page Closing duplicates. **C04**: close integrates causal-role model rather than preserving historiographical plurality.

## Instructional vs editorial architecture

| Layer | Role in the model |
| ----- | ----------------- |
| **Instructional** | Especially **EQ1**, **EQ3**, and the warrant half of **EQ5** |
| **Editorial** | Especially **EQ2**, **EQ4**, **EQ6**, **EQ7**, **EQ8**, and the integration half of **EQ5** |

Both layers are required. Editorial failure explains mediocrity-despite-correctness when EQ1 holds. EQ1 failure is instructional even if the prose is locally well authored.
