## Central question (answered for this sprint)

> How do we embed the validated Sprint 71 principles into Prism so that high-quality instructional design is produced by default, authors are asked only for information the system genuinely needs, and discipline-specific evidence can be supplied and retained where Prism cannot generate it appropriately?

**Sprint 72 answer:** Productise Evidence-Centred Learning at activity level inside the existing pipeline (selective evidence; simulated + conversation-attachment paths; guided-review; bridges; presentation refinements) without a new stage/page type. Large unfinished streams live in the product backlog.

## Planning principle (post-sprint)
