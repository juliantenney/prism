## F. Overall synthesis (frozen §F)

### What is working?

- Clear multi-level causal teaching: vulnerability / trigger / July decisions / non-inevitability.  
- Selective emphasis against naïve cause-lists and mechanical inevitability (within the delivered arc).  
- Purpose-fit figures for that causal-role model (D5 SATISFACTORY for actual journey).  
- Accessible prose carrying essential meaning (D15/D6 stance retained).  
- Avoids empty “many causes, equally weighted” retreat **within** its own model.

### Does the resource answer the intellectual question it was actually asked?

**No.** Asked why **historians disagree** and how **same evidence** supports **different weightings**. Delivered why/how **war began** under one **interactive multi-level causal model**.

### What most limits it from feeling like a high-quality authored chapter?

Two layers: (1) **instructional purpose misfit** relative to the commission (dominant for this case); (2) familiar **editorial** furniture — orientation prospectus, dual closure, flat hierarchy, residual restatement.

### Does its main weakness lie in instructional architecture, editorial architecture, or both?

**Both**, with a case-specific shift: instructional weakness here is **purpose-fit / epistemic form**, not merely weak internal sequencing. Editorial concerns remain but are not the distinctive Case 04 finding.

### What does this interpretive case reveal that Cases 01–03 could not?

PRISM can produce an **internally excellent answer to a neighbouring brief** (multi-level causal explanation) while **missing the disciplinary epistemic object** requested (historiographical disagreement / weighting). Earlier cases did not pressure preservation of interpretive plurality.

### Which findings now have cross-case evidence?

See §G.

### Which previous hypotheses have been qualified?

- “Instructional architecture is stronger than editorial architecture” — **qualified / not preservable as a four-case generalisation**. Case 04 shows instructional **CONCERN** on purpose-fit while remaining coherent for an alternate brief.  
- Assumption that multi-causal coherence ≈ interpretive success — **rejected** for this brief.

### What requires C05 or later investigation?

- Whether abstract conceptual briefs (C05) also drift toward adjacent, more “teachable” spines.  
- Where in the pipeline (EJP/XD…) interpretive / disagreement briefs are flattened into single-model explanations (WP4 — not this task).  
- T-007 synthesis across full set; do not universalise from four cases.  
- Candidate model/instrument observation on preserving disciplinary epistemic form (below) — do not revise P1–P11 yet.
