## Significant findings

**Strengths**

- Coherent six-resource educational progression.  
- Authentic application of evidence evaluation and decision-making.  
- Transfer from supported library scenarios to an independent school traffic-management assessment.  
- Explicit C5→C6 learner-response handoff (also separately verified via live-browser testing outside this QA instrument).  
- Generally appropriate scaffolding and feedback.

**Qualifications**

- C4 contains pre-response guidance that may anticipate part of the learner’s comparative interpretation — confirmed minor editorial issue; not an assessment blocker.  
- Some repeated explanatory material across C2–C4 may increase reading burden (also supports standalone use).  
- Formal accessibility assurance and wider runtime verification are **separate** from educational QA.

**Verification behaviour**

- Stage 2 challenged findings rather than automatically endorsing them.  
- One run rejected an unsupported activity-count discrepancy.  
- Another identified the C4 guidance issue and adjusted the scaffolding rating.
