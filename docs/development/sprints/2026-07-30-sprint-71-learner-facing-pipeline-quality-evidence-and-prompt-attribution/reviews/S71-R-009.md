## Controlled-experiment / architectural note

Earlier Sprint 71 resources used relatively generic briefs. This resource used a substantially richer brief explicitly requiring source analysis, provenance, competing interpretations, evidence weighting, historical judgement and diagnostic reasoning.

**Result:** Generated quality improved significantly — evidence that generation quality is responsive to richer instructional briefs.

**Remaining gap:** Implementation still tends to *describe* evidence more effectively than it *enables* learners to interrogate evidence directly. Limitation appears **architectural** (Evidence-Based Learning / Evidence-Centred Activity Design) rather than purely prompt-related.

Logged as workflow/architecture observation `S71-O-006` (not an instructional Improvement Register defect duplicate of `S71-F-001`).
